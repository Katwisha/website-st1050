Your details.
Handled with care.

This notice explains what happens when you use THE HUB's contact and enquiry forms.

Information you choose to share

Contact and enquiry forms may ask for your name, email address, optional phone number, enquiry topic, preferred date, project or group name, and message. Please do not include passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information.

Why we use it

THE HUB uses submitted details to understand and respond to your message, discuss an enquiry, or follow up about a potential event or collaboration. Submitting a form does not confirm availability, a booking, or participation.

Form delivery and retention

When deployed with Netlify Forms, your submission is processed by Netlify and made available to THE HUB for response. Email notifications must be configured in the hosting account. If online delivery is unavailable, the form offers an email draft addressed to katakanna18@gmail.com; you decide whether to send it.

Submissions should be retained only as long as needed to respond and meet applicable obligations. Actual access and retention depend on the hosting account's settings and should be reviewed by the site operator.

Analytics and third-party content

This version does not load advertising or analytics trackers. The map is embedded from OpenStreetMap and may receive technical connection data when it loads. Your browser can block the map; a direct map link is also provided.

Your choices

You may contact THE HUB to ask about a message you submitted or request that it be deleted, subject to applicable requirements. Email katakanna18@gmail.com.

This concise notice is a practical starting point, not legal advice. The site operator should confirm the final policy, hosting configuration, and applicable privacy obligations before launch.
